About Asma-ul-Husna

Asma-ul-Husna means the beautiful names of Allah. Each name reflects one of His beautiful attributes and helps us remember and understand His greatness.

This website is created as a simple and peaceful way to explore the 99 Names, their meanings, and their recitation.
